# Offer Rules

A beginner's offer must be easy to understand, easy to say yes to, and honest about having few or no results.

## The five parts

1. **The offer (one sentence).** [Outcome] for [ICP] in [time or scope], [how risk is removed].
2. **What they get.** The concrete deliverable. Things they can see or touch: a setup, a page, a flow, a report, a number of videos.
3. **Price.** From the market check. A beginner can price at the low to middle of what others charge. Always give a number or a tight range.
4. **First deal structure.** How the first client starts with little risk.
5. **Why trust you.** One line from advantage-mining. Only proof or experience. Never invented.

## Outcome rules

- One outcome, not three.
- Measurable or visible: bookings, replies, recovered leads, hours saved, reviews, site speed.
- Never promise revenue numbers the beginner can't back.
- Use the buyer's words ("missed calls", "no shows", "abandoned carts"), not marketer words ("conversion optimization").

## First deal structures for beginners

Pick one that fits the niche:

| Structure | Use when | Example |
|---|---|---|
| Pilot | The service runs over time | "First 2 weeks at half price, keep going only if it works." |
| Pay after it works | The result is clear and fast | "Pay once the missed call text back is live and catching calls." |
| Fixed small first project | The buyer needs to see quality | "One landing page for your current ad, fixed price, 5 days." |
| Free audit with a specific finding | The problem is visible from outside | "I'll send you a 3 minute video showing where your site loses bookings." |
| Performance based | Results are countable | "Paid per booked appointment for the first month." |

Avoid giving the full service free. Free work with no path to paid attracts the wrong buyers.

## Why trust you line

| They have | Line looks like |
|---|---|
| Proof | "Set this up for [type of business], which [result]." |
| Experience | "Worked [time] inside [industry/role], so I know [specific problem]." |
| Skill only | Skip the claim. Let the first deal carry trust: "You only pay once it's working." |

## Before and after

Before: "I help businesses grow with AI automation."
After: "I set up a missed call text back for physio clinics so callers you miss get a booking link in 60 seconds. Pay once it's live."

Before: "Social media management for brands."
After: "8 short videos a month for independent coffee roasters on Shopify, made from footage you already have. First month at half price."

## Banned in the offer

"scale", "grow your business", "10x", "leverage", "unlock", "streamline", "cutting edge", "revolutionize", "done for you solutions", "take it to the next level", "boost your revenue", any stat not backed by the user.
