# Advantage Mining

The goal: find why one group of buyers would trust THIS beginner over a random freelancer with the same skill. The edge decides the niche. The niche decides everything else.

## Where the edge hides (check in this order)

1. **Past jobs.** Three years as a dental receptionist beats any certificate when selling to dental clinics. They know the software, the words, the daily mess.
2. **Industries known from inside.** Family business, part-time jobs, college internships. A kid who grew up in a parent's restaurant knows restaurant owners.
3. **Deep tool knowledge.** Not "knows AI". Specific: "built 12 GoHighLevel funnels", "Shopify Flow", "Zoho CRM". Tool users cluster in industries.
4. **Language and location.** Speaks the buyer's language, same country, same time zone, same city.
5. **Small wins.** Grew their own Instagram, fixed a friend's website, automated their college club's signups. Small but true.
6. **Personal interests with buyer overlap.** A gym regular selling to gym owners. A gamer selling to gaming studios.
7. **Access.** A network in a group (alumni, community, family trade).

## Labels

Tag every item before using it:

- **Proof:** a result with an outcome ("got a friend's salon 40 bookings from Instagram").
- **Experience:** time spent inside an industry or role ("2 years at a logistics company").
- **Skill:** what they can do ("builds Make.com automations").

Proof and experience can go in the "Why trust you" line. Skill alone can't carry trust, so pair it with experience or use a risk-free first deal.

## Turn vague skills into tasks

Buyers buy tasks, not skills.

| Vague skill | Concrete tasks |
|---|---|
| AI automation | missed call text back, lead routing, invoice data entry, review reply drafts, appointment reminders |
| Social media | short video editing, content calendar, comment replies, UGC sourcing |
| Web design | booking page, landing page for ads, site speed fix, Google Business profile |
| Marketing | Meta ads management, email flows for Shopify, Google reviews growth |
| Virtual assistant | inbox cleanup, CRM updates, calendar booking, supplier follow ups |

## Pick the strongest edge

Rank by: how much trust it creates for a specific buyer group, and whether that group pays for the skill. An edge the buyer can feel in one sentence wins.

Weak: "I'm a fast learner and passionate about AI."
Strong: "I worked the front desk at a physio clinic for 2 years, so I know how many leads die in voicemail."

## If nothing is found

Say nothing about it in the output. Use the niche with the most visible pain and easiest list building, and make the offer carry the trust through risk reversal.
